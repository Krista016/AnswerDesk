ABS & Traction Control Lights On: Wheel Speed Sensor Wire Chafing Fix

Written & Audited by Robert Chen Master Automotive Technician (ASE) • AnswerDesk Vetted Expert • Updated November 2026
⚡ Quick Expert Answer • Automotive & OBD-II Diagnostics

Read ABS module wheel speed sensor live data, inspect reluctor tone rings, and repair severed wiring harnesses.

Pre-Fix Diagnostic Checklist

  • ✓Scan ABS module for C-series chassis trouble codes (e.g. C0035, C0040)
  • ✓Inspect wheel speed sensor harness near strut and suspension joints
  • ✓Clean metallic brake dust off magnetic reluctor tone ring on wheel hub
  • ✓Write down the exact error message, flashing LED pattern, or fault code displayed before restarting the device.

Diagnostic & Troubleshooting Protocol

Read ABS module wheel speed sensor live data, inspect reluctor tone rings, and repair severed wiring harnesses.

Step 1
Scan Wheel Speed Live Data

All 4 sensors should read identical MPH. If one sensor suddenly drops to 0 MPH while cruising, that sensor circuit has a broken wire or cracked tone ring.

Drive vehicle while graphing all 4 wheel speed sensors
Step 2
Inspect Wiring Harness at Suspension Pivot

Suspension compression and steering articulation fatigue the internal copper wires, creating intermittent open circuits over bumps.

Inspect cable clipped to front strut tower and knuckle
Step 3
Inspect Wheel Bearing Hub Reluctor Ring

A rusted or chipped tone ring creates erratic signal frequencies that trigger false ABS activation at low speeds.

Inspect magnetic tone ring embedded in wheel bearing seal
Expert Caution / Safety Note: Normal power-assisted braking remains functional when the ABS light is on, but anti-lock skid prevention and stability control are disabled.
