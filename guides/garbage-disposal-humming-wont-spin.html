Garbage Disposal Humming But Not Spinning: Manual Hex Key Release Fix

Written & Audited by Michael Torres Master Appliance Technician • AnswerDesk Vetted Expert • Updated November 2026
⚡ Quick Expert Answer • Home Appliances & Refrigeration

Dislodge jammed bone or fruit pit using under-sink 1/4-inch hex socket and reset red thermal overload button.

Pre-Fix Diagnostic Checklist

  • ✓Turn off disposal wall switch immediately to prevent motor burnout
  • ✓Insert 1/4-inch Allen hex wrench into center bottom socket
  • ✓Press red thermal reset button on bottom of disposal casing
  • ✓Write down the exact error message, flashing LED pattern, or fault code displayed before restarting the device.

Diagnostic & Troubleshooting Protocol

Dislodge jammed bone or fruit pit using under-sink 1/4-inch hex socket and reset red thermal overload button.

Step 1
Turn Off Wall Switch

Continuous humming without rotation draws locked-rotor amperage that overheats the stator motor windings.

Do not allow disposal to hum for more than 10 seconds
Step 2
Manually Free Jammed Shredder Plate

Insert 1/4-inch Allen wrench (or disposal jam wrench) and crank back and forth clockwise and counter-clockwise until plate spins 360 degrees freely.

Locate 1/4-inch hex socket in exact center of bottom motor casing under sink
Step 3
Reset Red Thermal Overload Switch

Firmly press the red button in until it clicks. Run cold water and test wall switch.

Feel for small square red button on bottom or lower side of unit
Expert Caution / Safety Note: Never stick hands or fingers into disposal hopper; use long kitchen tongs or pliers to retrieve dislodged glass, bones, or metal bottle caps.

How to Verify the Fix Worked

  1. Restart the affected system or device completely and confirm normal startup without returning warnings.
  2. Test the specific function that previously failed under normal everyday operating conditions.
  3. Monitor for 24 hours to verify the fault code or symptom does not recur.
