Dog Ingested Chocolate: Toxicity Risk Chart by Weight & Emergency Triage

Diagnostic & Troubleshooting Protocol

Calculate theobromine toxicity dosage (baker's chocolate vs milk chocolate), symptoms timeline, and hydrogen peroxide protocol.

Step 1
Calculate Theobromine Dosage by Chocolate Type

Toxic threshold in dogs: 20 mg/kg causes agitation and vomiting; 40-50 mg/kg causes cardiac arrhythmias; 60 mg/kg causes seizures and potential death.

Cocoa powder: 400-700mg/oz; Baker's chocolate: 400mg/oz; Dark chocolate: 150mg/oz; Milk chocolate: 50mg/oz
Step 2
Observe Symptoms Timeline (2 to 12 Hours)

Watch for severe panting, pacing, excessive thirst, vomiting, diarrhea, rapid irregular heartbeat, muscle tremors, and seizures.

Symptoms typically develop within 6 to 12 hours of ingestion
Step 3
When Is Inducing Vomiting Safe?

3% Hydrogen Peroxide (1 teaspoon per 5 lbs of body weight, max 3 tablespoons) can be given orally. NEVER induce vomiting if dog is already showing neurological symptoms, is lethargic, or is a brachycephalic breed (Bulldog, Pug).

ONLY under veterinary direction and within 2 hours of ingestion
Expert Caution / Safety Note: White chocolate contains virtually zero theobromine (negligible toxicity risk), but its high fat content can still trigger severe acute pancreatitis.
