Why did Amazon close my account for "excessive returns" or "Concessions Abuse" and can I appeal?

Written & Audited by Rebecca Turner Prime Billing & Order Resolution Expert • AnswerDesk Vetted Expert • Updated November 2026
⚡ Quick Expert Answer • Amazon Account Locked, On Hold, OTP/2FA, Hacked Account & Verification Loops

Amazon's OFM (Order Fulfillment & Concessions) team flags accounts with high return ratios, frequent "Delivered Not Received" refunds, or returned items mismatched from serial numbers. Reply directly to the ofm@amazon.com notice with calm, factual order IDs and carrier drop-off receipts explaining legitimate defects or porch theft—avoid opening duplicate accounts using the same card/address, which triggers automatic closure.

Before You Start: Quick Pre-Check

  • ✓Sign in directly at amazon.com (avoid links in text messages or emails) and verify you are logged into the exact account associated with the order or Prime membership.
  • ✓Have your 17-digit Order ID (e.g., 112-xxxxxxx-xxxxxxx), device serial number (DSN), or Prime Video error code ready.
  • ✓For Fire TV, Echo, Kindle, or Ring devices, confirm your Wi-Fi router is online and temporarily disable VPNs or custom DNS blockers.

Why This Issue Happens

Amazon's OFM (Order Fulfillment & Concessions) team flags accounts with high return ratios, frequent "Delivered Not Received" refunds, or returned items mismatched from serial numbers.

This issue falls under Amazon Account Locked, On Hold, OTP/2FA, Hacked Account & Verification Loops (Amazon, Prime & Smart Devices). Confirming the exact trigger before making changes prevents unnecessary resets and helps preserve your data and account standing.

Step-by-Step Resolution Protocol

Step 1
Primary Resolution Step

Reply directly to the ofm@amazon.com notice with calm, factual order IDs and carrier drop-off receipts explaining legitimate defects or porch theft—avoid opening duplicate accounts using the same card/address, which triggers automatic closure.

How to Verify the Fix Worked

  1. Check Your Orders, Manage Your Content and Devices, or Prime Membership settings to confirm the status change or device registration shows accurately.
  2. Power-cycle the Amazon device (unplug for 30 seconds) and test streaming, Wi-Fi connectivity, or Alexa voice response.
  3. Save any refund confirmation email, return drop-off receipt, or A-to-z Guarantee claim reference number until funds post to your original payment method.

What if the issue still persists? If an order claim, locked account appeal, or unauthorized charge is not resolved through automated self-service, open Amazon Customer Service directly from the official Help & Customer Service menu inside your account to request expert review with your Order ID.

⚠️ Expert Safety & Verification Advisory: Anti-Scam Warning: Amazon will never call asking you to buy gift cards, install remote-access software, or read out a one-time passcode (OTP) to "cancel an unauthorized order." Always verify orders and messages solely inside Your Account > Message Center at amazon.com.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will fixing "Amazon close my account for "excessive returns" or "Concessions Abuse" and can I appeal" delete my personal files, data, or account history?

Deregistering and re-registering a Fire TV, Kindle, or Echo device does not delete your purchased digital books, Prime Video library, or account history—everything tied to your Amazon account syncs back once you sign in again.

How long does it normally take to resolve this Amazon, Prime & Smart Devices issue?

Device deregistration, DNS changes, and app cache clears fix playback/Wi-Fi issues within 5–10 minutes. Amazon account lock appeals typically receive a response within 24–48 hours, and card refunds normally appear in 3–5 business days.

What should I do if the problem returns after following these steps?

If an order claim, locked account appeal, or unauthorized charge is not resolved through automated self-service, open Amazon Customer Service directly from the official Help & Customer Service menu inside your account to request expert review with your Order ID.
