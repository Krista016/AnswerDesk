'Mobile Network Not Available' / 'No SIM Card' Error on Android Fix

Written & Audited by Elena Rostova Mobile OS & Telecommunications Expert • AnswerDesk Vetted Expert • Updated November 2026
⚡ Quick Expert Answer • Mobile & Android Devices

Re-seat physical SIM card tray, toggle Airplane Mode network stack, and reset Access Point Names (APN).

Pre-Fix Diagnostic Checklist

  • ✓Toggle Airplane Mode ON for 30 seconds, then OFF
  • ✓Remove SIM card and clean gold contacts with pencil eraser
  • ✓Reset Access Point Names (APN) to default carrier profile
  • ✓Write down the exact error message, flashing LED pattern, or fault code displayed before restarting the device.

Diagnostic & Troubleshooting Protocol

Re-seat physical SIM card tray, toggle Airplane Mode network stack, and reset Access Point Names (APN).

Step 1
Clean SIM Card Contacts

Fingerprint oils and pocket dust on contact pads disrupt the high-speed serial bus between the SIM card and baseband modem.

Eject SIM tray with paperclip > Wipe gold contact pads with clean microfiber cloth
Step 2
Reset Network Settings

Clears corrupted carrier PRL (Preferred Roaming List) roaming profiles and cellular modem registrations.

Settings > General Management > Reset > Reset Network Settings
Step 3
Restore Default APN Settings

Downloads the verified carrier cellular APN profile required for VoLTE and mobile data handshakes.

Settings > Connections > Mobile networks > Access Point Names > Three dots menu > Reset to default
Expert Caution / Safety Note: If switching to a newly purchased phone, contact your carrier to ensure your physical SIM or eSIM has been provisioned on their 5G network.

How to Verify the Fix Worked

  1. Restart the affected system or device completely and confirm normal startup without returning warnings.
  2. Test the specific function that previously failed under normal everyday operating conditions.
  3. Monitor for 24 hours to verify the fault code or symptom does not recur.
