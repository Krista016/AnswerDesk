Top-Load Washing Machine Shaking Violently & Banging on Spin Cycle Fix

Written & Audited by Michael Torres Master Appliance Technician • AnswerDesk Vetted Expert • Updated November 2026
⚡ Quick Expert Answer • Home Appliances & Refrigeration

Diagnose worn suspension damper spring rods, broken tub dampening straps, and unlevel leveling legs.

Pre-Fix Diagnostic Checklist

  • ✓Perform the 'Bounce Test' on inner tub (should settle immediately without rebounding)
  • ✓Inspect leveling legs with bubble level
  • ✓Replace all 4 suspension rods as a matched set
  • ✓Write down the exact error message, flashing LED pattern, or fault code displayed before restarting the device.

Diagnostic & Troubleshooting Protocol

Diagnose worn suspension damper spring rods, broken tub dampening straps, and unlevel leveling legs.

Step 1
The Tub Bounce Test

A healthy suspension stops immediately. If the tub bounces up and down 2-4 times like a pogo stick, the internal hydraulic friction dampening pads inside the suspension rods are completely worn out.

Firmly push down on center of wash tub and release quickly
Step 2
Inspect Leveling Feet

Turn adjustable threaded feet until cabinet has zero wobble on all four corners, then tighten locking jam nuts.

Place bubble level across front and side edges of washer cabinet
Step 3
Replace Suspension Rod Set

Always replace all 4 rods simultaneously (Part W10780048); replacing only 1 rod leads to unbalanced weight transfer.

Pop open top console lid > Support tub > Replace rods one by one
Expert Caution / Safety Note: Apply heavy silicone dampening grease to the upper suspension ball cups to eliminate squeaking and friction sticking.

How to Verify the Fix Worked

  1. Restart the affected system or device completely and confirm normal startup without returning warnings.
  2. Test the specific function that previously failed under normal everyday operating conditions.
  3. Monitor for 24 hours to verify the fault code or symptom does not recur.
