Why does my PayPal payment say "Pending" instead of "Completed"?

Written & Audited by Victoria Price Fintech Billing & Account Recovery Expert • AnswerDesk Vetted Expert • Updated November 2026
⚡ Quick Expert Answer • Funds on Hold, 21-Day Seller Holds, Pending Payments & eChecks

A Pending payment means either the sender paid via eCheck (awaiting bank clearing), the recipient's email address isn't confirmed/registered with PayPal yet, or the transaction is undergoing an automated 24–72 hour Payment Review for fraud screening. Never ship physical goods while a payment status reads Pending or Under Review.

Before You Start: Quick Pre-Check

  • ✓Access PayPal directly by typing paypal.com into a standard browser (disable VPNs, proxies, and ad-blockers that trigger security flags).
  • ✓Gather transaction IDs, carrier tracking numbers, bank statement descriptors, or unexpired government ID if requested in Resolution Center.
  • ✓Check your verified email inbox and PayPal Message Center for exact notice dates and compliance ticket numbers.

Why This Issue Happens

A Pending payment means either the sender paid via eCheck (awaiting bank clearing), the recipient's email address isn't confirmed/registered with PayPal yet, or the transaction is undergoing an automated 24–72 hour Payment Review for fraud screening.

This issue falls under Funds on Hold, 21-Day Seller Holds, Pending Payments & eChecks (PayPal & Digital Payments). Confirming the exact trigger before making changes prevents unnecessary resets and helps preserve your data and account standing.

Step-by-Step Resolution Protocol

Step 1
Primary Resolution Step

Never ship physical goods while a payment status reads Pending or Under Review.

How to Verify the Fix Worked

  1. Open PayPal > Activity and Resolution Center to confirm no remaining "Action Required" tasks or open document requests remain.
  2. Verify that your funding source (bank account or debit/credit card) shows as Confirmed in Wallet with an exact billing address match.
  3. For holds or refunds, check the expected release date displayed inside the transaction detail screen and confirm email confirmation arrived from service@paypal.com.

What if the issue still persists? If automated review stalls past the stated SLA (3–5 business days for limitation reviews or 24 hours post-delivered tracking for 21-day holds), message PayPal Support inside the official PayPal Message Center with your case ID and keep screenshots of all submitted proof.

⚠️ Expert Safety & Verification Advisory: Anti-Scam Warning: Never call phone numbers pasted inside PayPal invoice notes, "money request" descriptions, or urgent emails claiming an unauthorized charge. Never install remote desktop apps (AnyDesk/TeamViewer) for anyone claiming to be PayPal support, and never share 2FA SMS codes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will fixing "my PayPal payment say "Pending" instead of "Completed"" delete my personal files, data, or account history?

Following official Resolution Center steps, confirming cards in Wallet, or updating carrier tracking will not close your account or lose funds. Even on a permanent limitation, legitimate cleared balances are subject to PayPal's statutory 180-day reserve release window.

How long does it normally take to resolve this PayPal & Digital Payments issue?

Card confirmation and billing updates take effect immediately. PayPal 21-day holds typically release 1 day after confirmed carrier delivery; KYC limitation document reviews usually take 2–5 business days; card refunds take 3–5 business days to post to your bank.

What should I do if the problem returns after following these steps?

If automated review stalls past the stated SLA (3–5 business days for limitation reviews or 24 hours post-delivered tracking for 21-day holds), message PayPal Support inside the official PayPal Message Center with your case ID and keep screenshots of all submitted proof.
