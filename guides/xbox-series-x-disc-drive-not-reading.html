Xbox Series X / One Disc Drive Not Reading Games or Clicking Noise Fix

Written & Audited by Tyler Brooks Console Hardware & Micro-Soldering Tech • AnswerDesk Vetted Expert • Updated November 2026
⚡ Quick Expert Answer • Gaming Consoles & Systems

Clean optical laser lens assembly, lubricate drive tray guide rails, and replace worn laser pickup (HOP-B150).

Pre-Fix Diagnostic Checklist

  • ✓Clean laser lens using optical cleaning disc or isopropyl alcohol cotton swab
  • ✓Hold disc lightly for 1 second during insertion to force optical feeder engagement
  • ✓Replace optical pickup laser head module
  • ✓Write down the exact error message, flashing LED pattern, or fault code displayed before restarting the device.

Diagnostic & Troubleshooting Protocol

Clean optical laser lens assembly, lubricate drive tray guide rails, and replace worn laser pickup (HOP-B150).

Step 1
The 'Hold Disc for 1 Second' Feeder Trick

Forces the internal optical laser carriage to seat at the inner track zero position before reading Table of Contents.

Insert disc into drive slot > Hold back of disc with finger for 1 second until motor pulls firmly > Release disc
Step 2
Clean Optical Laser Pickup Lens

Dust film on the laser lens diffracts the 405nm blue laser light, preventing read verification on high-density dual-layer Blu-ray discs.

Disassemble drive casing > Locate small glass objective lens > Clean gently with 99% IPA cotton swab
Step 3
Daughterboard Marriage Warning

The optical drive PCB is cryptographically paired to the Xbox motherboard; swapping drives without transferring the original board prevents all games from launching.

Never swap the entire disc drive from another Xbox without moving the original daughterboard
Expert Caution / Safety Note: Discs with oily fingerprints or deep circular scratch rings cannot be read; clean with microfiber cloth from center outward in straight lines.
