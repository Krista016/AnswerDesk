How do I remove an active infostealer/trojan after accidentally running a fake cracked software or PDF .exe file?

Written & Audited by Katherine Moore Cybersecurity & Antivirus Diagnostics Lead • AnswerDesk Vetted Expert • Updated November 2026
⚡ Quick Expert Answer • Kaspersky / UltraAV Migration, ESET, Webroot, Trend Micro & Cross-Antivirus Issues

Immediately disconnect the PC from Wi-Fi/Ethernet so credentials aren't further exfiltrated. From a separate clean device, change passwords and revoke active sessions (log out everywhere) for Google, Microsoft, Apple, banking, PayPal, and Discord—infostealers steal live browser cookies bypassing 2FA! Then wipe and clean-install Windows from a clean USB rather than trusting partial cleanup.

Before You Start: Quick Pre-Check

  • ✓Confirm only ONE real-time antivirus product is installed on the computer (running two real-time engines simultaneously causes crashes, update errors, and 100% disk/CPU spikes).
  • ✓Locate your active subscription email or 25-character license key inside your official vendor account portal before running a removal/reinstall tool.
  • ✓Close open browsers and save work, as official antivirus cleanup utilities require a full system reboot.

How to Verify the Fix Worked

  1. Reboot your PC or Mac and confirm the security suite launches cleanly in the system tray / menu bar with a green "Protected" status.
  2. Run a manual Virus Definition Update and a Quick Scan to verify the real-time protection engine, firewall, and cloud lookup services connect without errors.
  3. Open Windows Security > Virus & Threat Protection > Manage Providers (or macOS System Settings > Privacy & Security) to confirm no conflicting legacy AV driver remains.

What if the issue still persists? If installation or uninstall loops persist after running the vendor's official removal utility (e.g., NRnR, MCPR, Avast Clear), boot into Windows Safe Mode to run the cleanup tool offline, then reinstall a fresh installer downloaded directly from the vendor's primary domain.

⚠️ Expert Safety & Verification Advisory: Anti-Scam Warning: Ignore fake browser pop-ups claiming "Your PC is infected!" and fraudulent PDF renewal emails claiming "$399.99 was auto-charged for Geek/Norton/McAfee." Never call phone numbers in unsolicited emails—manage subscriptions only by logging directly into your official vendor account.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will fixing "remove an active infostealer/trojan after accidentally running a fake cracked software…" delete my personal files, data, or account history?

Uninstalling, repairing, or switching antivirus software does not delete your personal files, photos, or documents. Before removing a suite, restore any mistakenly quarantined safe files from Quarantine if needed.

How long does it normally take to resolve this Antivirus & Endpoint Security issue?

Running an official removal utility, rebooting, and installing a fresh antivirus build typically takes 10–20 minutes. Subscription auto-renewal cancellations take effect immediately in your vendor account portal.

What should I do if the problem returns after following these steps?
