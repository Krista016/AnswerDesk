Bosch Washing Machine Error Code E18: Drain Pump Blockage Protocol

Written & Audited by Michael Torres Master Appliance Technician • AnswerDesk Vetted Expert • Updated November 2026
⚡ Quick Expert Answer • Home Appliances & Refrigeration

Clear coin trap filter debris, release jammed pump impeller, and test drain hose check valve.

Pre-Fix Diagnostic Checklist

  • ✓Open lower circular access flap on bottom front right
  • ✓Drain water via pull-out emergency drain hose
  • ✓Unscrew and clean the main drain pump filter basket
  • ✓Write down the exact error message, flashing LED pattern, or fault code displayed before restarting the device.

Diagnostic & Troubleshooting Protocol

Clear coin trap filter debris, release jammed pump impeller, and test drain hose check valve.

Step 1
Drain Standing Drum Water

Prevents gallons of soapy water from flooding floor when unscrewing the main filter.

Pull out the small black rubber hose next to filter cap and drain into shallow tray
Step 2
Inspect Pump Impeller for Foreign Objects

Spin the impeller with your finger; it should turn with slight magnetic resistance. Clear hairpins, dental floss, or coins jammed in blades.

Use flashlight to inspect pump chamber inside filter opening
Step 3
Clear Drain Hose Standpipe Siphon

A drain hose inserted too deep into the standpipe creates a siphon that draws water out prematurely or blocks discharge.

Verify household drain standpipe is between 24 and 40 inches high
Expert Caution / Safety Note: Run an empty 90°C maintenance cottons cycle with specialized washing machine cleaner once a month to dissolve accumulated grease.

  2. Test the specific function that previously failed under normal everyday operating conditions.
