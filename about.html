Our Mission & Background

AnswerDesk was founded with a singular, resolute objective: to make high-quality, professional guidance instantly accessible to everyone, anywhere, 24/7. By diagnosing the issue first and presenting step-by-step guidance, we empower users with immediate answers and connect them to real, human specialists whenever deeper intervention is needed.

How We Vet Our Experts — Multi-Stage Verification

Our marketplace is built upon a fundamental trust standard: credentials are meaningful only when they are verified, authentic, and continuously monitored. We reject superficial self-declarations and enforce a comprehensive, 3-phase verification system before any expert is approved to answer questions on AnswerDesk.

1
Phase 1: Professional Verification

"Are you genuinely qualified to practice your profession?"

🆔 Identity Verification
Government-issued ID verification, selfie liveness checks, and cross-referencing against independent identity databases to prevent impersonation.
🎓 Education & Degree Audits
Direct confirmation of academic degrees, graduation credentials, and accredited institutional diplomas.
📜 License & Regulatory Standing
Active license checks with state and national licensing authorities (e.g. State Bar, Medical Boards, Veterinary Boards, ASE Certifications).
🔍 Background & Disciplinary Check
Screening for active sanctions, professional disciplinary actions, license suspensions, or regulatory restrictions.
2
Phase 2: Expertise & Specialty Verification

"Are you genuinely experienced and qualified in the specific areas you advise on?"

🎯 Structured Specialty Mapping
Experts are categorized by exact domain, specialty, and sub-specialty (e.g., Veterinary → Dogs → Dermatology) rather than broad generic labels.
📝 Clinical & Practical Assessments
Mandatory practical reasoning examinations testing situational logic, risk recognition, and troubleshooting methodologies.
⚠️ Mandatory Safety Screening
Rigorous evaluations on identifying medical emergencies, high-risk physical hazards, and circumstances requiring immediate 911 or hands-on referral.
🏅 4-Level Credential Hierarchy
Transparent badges from Level 1 (Professionally Verified) to Level 4 (Board-Certified Recognized Specialist) ensure no exaggerated claims.
3
Phase 3: Platform Quality Verification & Monitoring

"Do you consistently provide safe, useful, and professional answers on our platform?"

⏱️ Monitored Probation Period
Newly approved experts undergo 100% review over their initial 30 to 50 answers before full platform privileges are unlocked.
👥 Blind Peer Reviews
Routine, randomized case reviews conducted by senior certified practitioners in the respective specialization.
⭐ Customer Feedback & Escalations
Real-time analysis of satisfaction ratings, repeat visits, resolution efficiency, and customer escalations.
🔄 Ongoing Re-Verification
Mandatory periodic renewal checks for licenses, certifications, and professional standing. Expired credentials result in immediate suspension.
🛡️ Complete Standard Operating Procedure

Our Zero-Tolerance Depaneling Policy

Our commitment to user safety and satisfaction is absolute. Any expert who fails to uphold our standards — whether through inaccurate advice, unprofessional communication, attempts to solicit personal payments, or failure to recognize critical safety risks — is subjected to immediate review by our Quality Compliance Committee.

If a customer complaint is found legitimate, we take immediate action, including full customer refund and permanent depaneling (termination) of the expert from AnswerDesk. We retain only ethical, compassionate, and highly competent professionals who put our users first.

If you recently connected with an expert and were not 100% satisfied with their advice or conduct, please visit our Escalation Desk. Our senior compliance team personally reviews and resolves every ticket within 24 hours.
