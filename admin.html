Phone Routing

Set your Master Phone Number and choose whether it overrides every expert category or acts as the automatic fallback when a category has no assigned number or the issue is unidentified.

Leave blank to hide direct call buttons until your real line is ready.
If empty, AnswerDesk automatically formats the Master Number cleanly for display while dialing E.164.

How Routing Priority Works
  • 1. Master Override ON: Master Number overrides all expert categories across the site.
  • 2. Master Override OFF + Category Number Set: Dials that category's own phone number.
  • 3. Master Override OFF + No Category Number (or Unidentified): Automatically defaults to the Master Number.
