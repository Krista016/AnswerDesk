Privacy & Trust

Privacy Policy

We believe your privacy is fundamental. We collect only what is necessary to solve your problem, and we never sell your data.

🛡️ Our Strict Privacy Pledge

We collect only the technical details related to your specific problem (such as device model, OS version, or symptom descriptions). Neither AnswerDesk nor our experts will EVER ask for your private banking passwords, credit card numbers, or sensitive financial credentials over chat. Furthermore, we never charge prior to expert connection, and you only pay once a solution has been provided.

1. Information We Collect (Issue-Focused Only)

At AnswerDesk, we intentionally minimize data collection. We do not demand unnecessary personal dossiers or track your web activity outside of AnswerDesk. The information we request is strictly limited to diagnosing and resolving your inquiry:

  • Issue Diagnostic Details: Device model (e.g., iPhone 14, Dell XPS), operating system version, pet breed/age, vehicle make/year, appliance model, or specific error codes and symptoms you describe.
  • General Geographic Region: Country or state/province to ensure connection with an expert familiar with local laws, plumbing codes, or regional electrical standards.
  • Contact Information (Voluntary): Email address or telephone number solely for sending consultation transcripts, follow-up answers, or billing receipts.
🔒 Financial Credential Protection

Our experts are strictly prohibited from soliciting credit card numbers, CVVs, bank account credentials, seed phrases, or private keys directly in chat. Official billing transactions are processed through encrypted, PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways only after a solution is delivered.

2. How We Use Allowable Cookies & Local Storage

We respect your browser environment and only use allowable, essential cookies and browser storage:

  • Essential Session State: We use local session storage so your active chat conversation and expert connection do not reset if you accidentally refresh the page.
  • Expert De-Duplication: We save a lightweight local session token to rotate between our 35 verified expert profiles so you are not shown the same profile repeatedly.
  • Cookie Consent Preference: We record your consent choice so the banner does not repeatedly prompt you on future visits.
  • No Cross-Site Ad Tracking: We do not deploy third-party advertising cookies, fingerprinting scripts, or cross-site tracking beacons.

3. Zero Third-Party Data Sharing Policy

We maintain a strict policy: We do not sell, rent, monetize, trade, or share user personal data with third-party advertisers, data aggregators, or commercial marketing brokers.

Your technical questions and chat records remain strictly between you, our automated triage engine, and the assigned verified expert assisting you.

4. How We Use Analytics to Improve User Experience

To keep AnswerDesk efficient and reliable, we gather anonymized, aggregated platform performance metrics:

  • Category Volume Trends: Identifying which topics receive the highest volume of inquiries (e.g. Windows blue screens vs. refrigerator defrost cycles) to ensure adequate expert staffing.
  • Resolution & Response Times: Monitoring how fast users connect with a specialist and the average time required to provide an effective answer.
  • User Satisfaction Signals: Aggregating post-session feedback, ratings, and resolution success rates to evaluate expert quality and eliminate recurring pain points.

All analytical reporting is anonymized and never linked to personally identifiable individual profiles.

5. Data Storage, Security & Retention

We do not store or process sensitive personal data unnecessarily. Communication logs are safeguarded using industry-standard TLS encryption in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest. Logs are retained only as long as necessary to provide reference transcripts, resolve customer support escalations, and satisfy regulatory auditing obligations.

6. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA Compliant)

Regardless of where you reside, AnswerDesk provides comprehensive control over your information:

  • Right of Access: You may request a copy of the diagnostic information associated with your consultation sessions.
  • Right to Rectification: You may request corrections to any inaccurate contact information.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may request the permanent deletion of your chat history, transcripts, and account records.
  • Right to Opt-Out: Since we never sell your personal information, you are automatically protected from commercial data sales under California (CCPA) and European (GDPR) standards.

7. Contact Our Data Protection Team

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, wish to exercise your data protection rights, or have a data security inquiry, please contact our team:

  • Privacy Inquiries: privacy@answerdesk.com
  • General Support: support@answerdesk.com
  • Escalations: AnswerDesk Escalation Desk
